Cutview console

Point-in-time inventory from three independent streams

Streams

Each stream arrives on its own connector. Counts anchor; movements replay; notices overlay. They are never summed together.

Coverage declarations

A declared hole makes a cut degraded, not wrong. Missing declarations are disclosed as an unproven assumption.

Keys

Choose a sku/location to build a cut. The latest physical count is a claim at an instant, not a running balance.

Cutviews

Ledger

Append-only. Reversals and corrections are new rows; nothing is edited in place.

Reconstruct a cut

A cut is at an instant, optionally restricted to what was knowable at another instant. Both are recorded on the result.

Knowledge cutoff empty means “now”. Simulating an exit leaves checkpoints and publishes nothing.

Compare two cuts

Differences are attributed: movements, anchor revisions, or re-anchoring. The identity must close to zero or the diff reports it.

Record a physical count

Entering a count for an existing snapshot adds a revision. It never edits the ledger and never overwrites an earlier count.

Correct one ledger movement

A correction appends a reversal and a replacement. Restating keeps the original effective instant; forwarding posts both now.

Rebuild runs

A run checkpoints after every step. An aborted run has published nothing; resuming replays from its last checkpoint and must produce the same digest.
